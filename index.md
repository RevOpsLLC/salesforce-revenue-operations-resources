# A Practical Approach to Revenue Operations

Revenue Operations should connect the teams, processes, systems, and data responsible for acquiring, serving, retaining, and growing customers.

A typical customer lifecycle might look like:

**Lead → Qualification → Opportunity → Quote → Customer → Delivery → Billing → Payment → Retention → Expansion**

Organizations should be able to answer:

- Who owns each stage?
- Which system supports each stage?
- What information is required?
- Where is that information maintained?
- Where do handoffs occur?
- Which activities are automated?
- Where are employees performing manual work?
- How is performance measured?

Technology becomes significantly more valuable when these questions have clear answers.

---

# From Disconnected Systems to Governed Operations

Many organizations unintentionally build processes that look like:

**System A → Employee → Spreadsheet → Employee → System B**

Employees become the integration layer.

A stronger architecture moves toward:

**System A → Governed Integration → System B**

Across the broader technology environment, that can evolve into:

**Business Systems → Governed Integration → Salesforce / Customer Hub → Governed Data → Reporting, Analytics & AI**

The objective is not necessarily to put every business function into a single platform.

The objective is to establish clear systems of record, reliable integrations, trusted data, useful customer context, appropriate automation, and effective governance.

---

# Preparing for AI and Agentforce

AI should be part of the broader business and technology strategy rather than an isolated initiative.

Before introducing AI into a process, organizations should understand:

1. What business problem are we solving?
2. What process will AI support?
3. What information does AI need?
4. Can that information be trusted?
5. What actions should AI be allowed to perform?
6. What requires human approval?
7. How will exceptions be handled?
8. How will success be measured?

A useful foundation is:

**Process + Data + Context + Actions + Governance + Measurement**
